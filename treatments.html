All Treatments

Our Treatment Services

Comprehensive dental care from routine check-ups to advanced surgical procedures — all under one roof.

Teeth Cleaning & Scaling
General

Professional removal of plaque, tartar, and stains for optimal oral health. Recommended every 6 months.

Our hygienists use ultrasonic scalers and hand instruments to thoroughly clean above and below the gumline. Prevents gum disease, cavities, and bad breath.
Tooth Filling
General

Tooth-colored composite fillings that restore damaged teeth seamlessly and naturally.

We use modern composite resin that matches your natural tooth color. Decay is removed, the cavity cleaned, and filled — restoring function and aesthetics without mercury amalgam.
Tooth Extraction
Surgical

Safe, minimally-invasive extractions with effective pain management and rapid recovery.

Our surgeons perform simple and surgical extractions with precision. Local anesthesia ensures a comfortable, safe experience with minimal downtime.
Artificial Complete Denture
Prosthetic

Custom-crafted full dentures that restore your smile, confidence, and chewing function.

Our prosthodontists craft precision dentures using high-quality materials. Detailed impressions and expert dental lab work create dentures that look natural and fit comfortably.
Dental Implants
Surgical

Permanent, natural-looking tooth replacements surgically anchored in your jawbone.

Titanium posts surgically placed in the jawbone, topped with a crown. They look, feel, and function like natural teeth and with proper care can last a lifetime.
Laser Dentistry
General

Advanced laser treatments for precise, virtually painless dental procedures with faster healing.

Dental lasers are used for gum reshaping, cavity detection, whitening, and more. Reduces bleeding, swelling, and recovery time compared to traditional methods.
Root Canal Therapy
General

Painless root canal treatment to save infected teeth and provide lasting relief from pain.

We remove infected pulp, clean and shape the root canals, and seal them. A crown is placed afterward to protect the tooth. This saves your natural tooth from extraction.
Wisdom Tooth Extraction
Surgical

Expert removal of impacted wisdom teeth with minimal discomfort and quick recovery.

Impacted wisdom teeth cause pain, infection, and crowding. Our oral surgeons use digital X-rays to plan precise extractions for quick healing and minimal post-operative discomfort.
Fixed Partial Denture
Prosthetic

Dental bridges to replace missing teeth with permanent, natural-looking results.

A fixed partial denture (bridge) spans the gap of a missing tooth by anchoring to adjacent teeth. Restores appearance and function without the need for surgery or implants.
Teeth Whitening
Cosmetic

Professional whitening treatments for a noticeably brighter, more radiant smile.

We offer in-office and take-home whitening options. Professional-grade bleaching agents safely lighten teeth by several shades for a radiant, confident smile that lasts 6–24 months.
Veneers
Cosmetic

Ultra-thin porcelain shells for a flawless, Hollywood-worthy smile transformation.

Custom-made porcelain or composite shells bonded to the front surface of teeth. Correct chips, stains, gaps, and misalignment for a complete smile makeover with natural-looking results.
Pediatric Dentistry
Pediatric

Gentle, child-friendly dental care in a warm, welcoming and stress-free environment.

Our pediatric dentists specialize in caring for children from infancy through adolescence, creating a positive experience that builds healthy dental habits for life.
Flap Surgery
Surgical

Advanced periodontal surgery for treating severe gum disease and bone regeneration.

Involves folding back gum tissue to remove tartar deposits and repair bone damage caused by advanced gum disease. Reduces pocket depth and promotes healthy gum regeneration.
Orthodontic Braces
Orthodontic

Traditional and ceramic braces for a perfectly aligned, healthy, and beautiful smile.

We offer metal, ceramic, and self-ligating braces. Our orthodontists create customized treatment plans to straighten teeth and correct bite issues for all ages. Typically 18–24 months.
Clear Aligners
Orthodontic

Virtually invisible aligners for discreet, comfortable and effective teeth straightening.

Removable, transparent trays that gradually straighten teeth. Nearly invisible, more comfortable than braces, and allow normal eating and cleaning. Ideal for mild to moderate cases.
