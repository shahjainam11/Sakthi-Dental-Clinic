About Us

20+ Years of Trusted Dental Care

Sakthi Dental Clinic has been Hosur's most trusted dental destination since 2004. Founded by Dr. Anupriya, our clinic was built on a single promise — delivering compassionate, expert-led dental care to women, children, and families with honesty and warmth.

Over two decades, we've grown from a single-chair practice to a modern 10-chair multi-specialty clinic serving thousands of patients across Hosur and the greater Tamil Nadu region.

Implant Excellence

Dental Implants for a Permanent Solution

Our implantologist brings unparalleled expertise in dental implant surgery to Hosur. We use premium-grade titanium implants with a clinical success rate exceeding 98%.

Implants look, feel, and function exactly like your natural teeth — giving you back your confidence and quality of life permanently, without compromising adjacent teeth.

Our Journey

Two Decades of Excellence

From a single chair to a full multi-specialty clinic — our journey has always been guided by one goal: your best smile.

2004
The Beginning

Dr. Anupriya founded Sakthi Dental Clinic in Hosur with a single vision — providing compassionate, affordable dental care for every family in the region.

2008
Growing the Team

Expanded the clinic with advanced diagnostic equipment and recruited a growing team of dental specialists to offer comprehensive multi-specialty care.

2014
Technology Leap

Introduced laser dentistry, a dedicated pediatric care wing, and full orthodontic services including braces and clear aligners for all age groups.

2019
5000+ Smiles

Celebrated 5000+ satisfied patients and expanded to a modern 10-chair facility with dedicated spaces for each specialty.

2024
20 Years Strong

Completed 20 years of transforming smiles across Hosur and the greater Tamil Nadu region, with 9 specialist doctors and 15+ treatment services.
